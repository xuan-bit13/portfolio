02 — MOBILE UI/UX

LostNoMore

A lost-and-found app that streamlines reporting, searching and recovering belongings, with a clean UI, clear icons and readable labels that reduce cognitive load.

Card layouts, category chips and a fixed bottom bar with a quick-report button put core actions within 1–2 taps; item pages pair location info with built-in messaging.

Usability test (12 participants): 100% rated it clean and organized, 83.3% found forms easy, and 100% were open to using it in real life.
